# Code of Conduct

GAudio contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with empathy, patience, and technical clarity.
- Respect differing experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Keep examples, logs, and reports free of private credentials or media.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in any project space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the public contact method on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/guoxk-me). Include links or other context that helps the maintainer understand the incident. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and reporters will not be penalized for a good-faith report.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, warnings, comments, or other material that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, and any repeated pattern.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the GAudio project.
